# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (sample)

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly.
Unused days up to 5 may be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Expense reimbursement
Submit expense reports within 30 days. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
Travel must be booked through the approved company portal.

## Security
Use the company password manager and enable multi-factor authentication on all work accounts.
Report suspected phishing emails to the security team immediately.
